Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Your financial data stays on your device.

Effective Date

July 16, 2026

Welcome to HisabBook. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

HisabBook stores your financial information only on your device.

  • Income Records
  • Expense Records
  • Budget Information
  • Categories
  • Notes
  • Transaction History
✔ We do NOT upload your financial data to any server.

2. Local Data Storage

All financial records remain on your mobile device.

  • No cloud storage
  • No online database
  • No server backup
  • No access by the developer

If you uninstall the application or clear its data, your stored information may be permanently deleted.

3. How We Use Information

Your financial data never leaves your device.

If you contact us through email, we may use your email only to respond to your inquiry.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal financial information.

Since your financial data remains on your device, we do not have access to it.

5. Third-Party Services

HisabBook does not use any third-party service to collect or store your financial information.

6. Data Security

Your information is protected by your device's own security.

  • PIN
  • Password
  • Fingerprint
  • Face Unlock

We recommend enabling device security for maximum protection.

7. Children's Privacy

HisabBook is not intended for children under 13 years of age.

8. Your Rights

  • View your data
  • Edit your data
  • Delete your data
  • Uninstall the application anytime

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page.
